# 09. MULTI-UNIT RETAIL STORE OPERATIONS MANAGER
Company: MetroStyle Lifestyle Brands & Department Stores
Location: Full-Time / Store On-Site | ₹8L - ₹14L / $60k - $85k

### ABOUT THE ROLE
We are looking for a dynamic Retail Store Operations Manager to lead high-volume flagship store operations, oversee store merchandising, maximize sales revenue, manage inventory shrinkage, and lead a team of 40+ sales and cashier staff. You will own store P&L, customer NPS, and floor presentation standards.

### KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Drive overall retail store sales performance to meet and exceed monthly revenue and conversion rate targets (₹2Cr+ / $2M+ annual turnover).
- Manage complete store Profit & Loss (P&L), controlling operational payroll budgets, utility expenses, and visual merchandising costs.
- Recruit, train, schedule, and mentor retail floor staff, department leads, and cashiers, fostering an energetic sales environment.
- Oversee inventory receiving, stock replenishment, seasonal layout resets, planogram execution, and POS system accuracy.
- Enforce strict loss prevention protocols, physical security standards, and daily cycle counts to keep inventory shrinkage below 0.8%.
- Monitor retail KPIs: Footfall conversion rate, Average Transaction Value (ATV), Units Per Transaction (UPT), and Net Promoter Score (NPS).
- Ensure store presentation, cleanliness, lighting, pricing tags, and promotional signage conform to brand luxury guidelines.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS & COMPETENCIES
- 4 to 8 years in retail management, apparel/electronics department store supervision, or multi-unit store operations.
- Experience managing retail store P&L, inventory audits, and team scheduling software.
- Hands-on mastery of Point of Sale (POS) and retail ERP systems (Shopify POS, Oracle Retail / Micros, Lightspeed, SAP POS).
- Strong leadership in driving team sales motivation, consultative selling, and customer delight.
- Exceptional visual merchandising judgment, inventory control, and conflict de-escalation skills.
