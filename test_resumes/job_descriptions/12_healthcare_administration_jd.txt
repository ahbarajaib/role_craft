# 12. CLINICAL PRACTICE & HEALTHCARE OPERATIONS ADMINISTRATOR
Company: St. Jude Specialty Healthcare Network
Location: Full-Time / On-site | ₹9L - ₹15L / $75k - $100k

### ABOUT THE ROLE
We are hiring a Clinical Practice & Healthcare Operations Administrator to oversee daily outpatient medical clinic operations, patient intake workflows, medical billing/coding compliance, and healthcare staff administration. You will manage physician schedules, ensure strict HIPAA regulatory compliance, and elevate patient satisfaction scores.

### KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Oversee daily operations across multi-specialty clinical practices, supervising medical receptionists, medical assistants, and intake coordinators.
- Enforce strict adherence to HIPAA patient privacy regulations, OSHA healthcare standards, and state health department licensing rules.
- Manage patient scheduling workflows, clinic check-in/check-out protocols, and insurance pre-authorization verifications.
- Supervise medical billing operations: insurance claims submission, denial management, co-pay collections, and ICD-10/CPT coding compliance.
- Maintain and configure Electronic Health Record (EHR) and Practice Management (PM) systems (Epic, Cerner, Athenahealth, eClinicalWorks).
- Administer clinical operational budgets, medical supply inventory replenishment, and biomedical equipment maintenance contracts.
- Monitor patient satisfaction scores, handle patient grievances with empathy, and optimize clinic patient wait times.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS & COMPETENCIES
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Healthcare Administration (MHA), Health Information Management, or Nursing/Business Administration.
- 3 to 7 years in medical clinic management, hospital administration, or outpatient healthcare facility operations.
- Deep expertise in HIPAA compliance, healthcare billing regulations, CMS guidelines, and medical terminology.
- Hands-on mastery of leading EHR/EMR platforms: Epic, Cerner, Athenahealth, or NextGen.
- Strong leadership in clinical staff scheduling, patient advocacy, and financial revenue cycle management (RCM).
- Compassionate communication and de-escalation skills in fast-paced medical environments.
