# 07. STRATEGIC SOURCING & SENIOR PROCUREMENT SPECIALIST
Company: PrimeCraft Industrial Manufacturing
Location: Full-Time | ₹11L - ₹17L / $80k - $105k

### ABOUT THE ROLE
We are seeking a Strategic Sourcing & Senior Procurement Specialist to direct direct/indirect material procurement, vendor negotiation, and supplier lifecycle management. You will design RFP/RFQ bidding strategies, conduct total cost of ownership (TCO) analyses, negotiate master service agreements (MSAs), and deliver significant cost-savings.

### KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Lead strategic category sourcing initiatives across direct raw materials, capital equipment, and indirect MRO services.
- Prepare, issue, and evaluate Request for Proposals (RFP), Request for Quotations (RFQ), and Request for Information (RFI).
- Negotiate pricing, payment terms (Net 60/90), delivery schedules, and penalty clauses in Master Service Agreements and Statements of Work (SOW).
- Conduct supplier risk assessments, financial health checks, and quarterly supplier performance scorecards.
- Deliver verifiable year-over-year cost savings (10-18%) through competitive bidding, bulk consolidation, and alternative vendor qualification.
- Oversee the Purchase Order (PO) approval lifecycle, three-way invoice matching, and procurement audit compliance.
- Partner with engineering, manufacturing, and inventory teams to ensure raw material availability without excess inventory buildup.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS & COMPETENCIES
- Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain, Business, Engineering, or Finance. CPSM, CIPS, or CSCP certification preferred.
- 4 to 8 years in strategic procurement, supplier management, or global contract negotiation.
- Hands-on experience with e-Procurement platforms: SAP Ariba, Coupa, Oracle Procurement Cloud, or Jaggaer.
- Strong financial modeling skills for Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), supplier cost-breakdown analysis, and price index benchmarking.
- Expert contract drafting, legal clause negotiation, and ethical procurement governance standards.
