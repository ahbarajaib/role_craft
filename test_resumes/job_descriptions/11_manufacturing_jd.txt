# 11. PLANT PRODUCTION & OPERATIONS SUPERVISOR
Company: Precision MetalWorks & Industrial Manufacturing
Location: Plant On-Site | Shifts | ₹8L - ₹14L / $65k - $90k

### ABOUT THE ROLE
We are seeking an experienced Plant Production & Operations Supervisor to oversee daily assembly line operations, machine output, shift personnel (50+ plant technicians), and workplace safety. You will drive continuous improvement initiatives (5S, Kaizen), minimize machine downtime (OEE), and enforce ISO 9001 quality compliance.

### KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Direct daily manufacturing shift operations, monitoring assembly line throughput, cycle times, and output targets.
- Ensure strict compliance with OSHA industrial safety regulations, machine guarding standards, and hazardous waste handling protocols.
- Lead 5S workplace organization, Total Productive Maintenance (TPM), and Kaizen continuous improvement initiatives on the shop floor.
- Optimize Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE), reducing equipment changeover times (SMED) and unplanned machinery downtime.
- Monitor product quality parameters, inspecting finished goods to maintain defect rates below 50 PPM and ensure ISO 9001 compliance.
- Manage shift scheduling, line balance allocation, operator cross-training, and plant timekeeping records.
- Partner with plant maintenance and engineering to troubleshoot pneumatic, hydraulic, and CNC mechanical breakdowns.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS & COMPETENCIES
- Diploma or Bachelor's degree in Mechanical, Industrial, Manufacturing Engineering, or equivalent shop-floor experience.
- 4 to 8 years in plant manufacturing, CNC/machining operations, assembly lines, or industrial fabrication environments.
- In-depth knowledge of Lean Manufacturing tools: 5S, Kaizen, Kanban, Root Cause Analysis (5-Why, Ishikawa Fishbone), and TPM.
- Familiarity with plant ERP/MES systems (SAP PP, Plex, Epicor) and production scheduling software.
- Strong mechanical and electrical troubleshooting comprehension, with a zero-compromise mindset for workplace safety.
