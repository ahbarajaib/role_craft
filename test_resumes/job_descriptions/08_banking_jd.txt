# 08. RETAIL & COMMERCIAL BANKING RELATIONSHIP MANAGER
Company: Heritage Global Bank | Wealth & Commercial Lending Group
Location: Full-Time | ₹10L - ₹16L + Performance Bonuses

### ABOUT THE ROLE
We are seeking an energetic and licensed Banking Relationship Manager to manage a portfolio of High-Net-Worth Individuals (HNIs) and SME commercial clients. You will provide strategic wealth advisory, underwrite loan applications, manage deposit portfolios, cross-sell investment and insurance products, and maintain strict regulatory KYC/AML compliance.

### KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Acquire, manage, and expand relationships with high-net-worth clients, business owners, and mid-market corporate accounts.
- Cross-sell financial products including mutual funds, portfolio management services (PMS), fixed deposits, corporate salary accounts, and commercial credit lines.
- Evaluate client creditworthiness, review balance sheets, tax returns, and collateral documentation for retail and commercial loan approvals.
- Ensure 100% adherence to central banking guidelines (RBI / OCC / FDIC), anti-money laundering (AML), and customer due diligence (KYC/CDD) policies.
- Conduct regular portfolio reviews to assess investment performance, asset allocation, and liquidity requirements.
- Achieve quarterly revenue targets across Net Interest Margin (NIM), fee-based income, and new asset acquisition (AUM growth).
- Resolve complex client banking inquiries, wire transfer authorizations, and foreign exchange (Forex) transactions.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS & COMPETENCIES
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Finance, Economics, Commerce, or Business Administration. Financial licensing (NISM, AMFI, IRDA, or CFP/Series 7) preferred.
- 3 to 7 years in retail branch banking, private wealth management, or commercial lending relationship management.
- Proficiency in core banking software solutions (Infosys Finacle, Oracle Flexcube, Temenos, or Salesforce Financial Services Cloud).
- Strong understanding of financial underwriting, credit risk analysis, debt-service coverage ratio (DSCR), and wealth advisory concepts.
- Proven track record of client acquisition, relationship nurturing, and quota achievement.
- High integrity, financial ethics, and compliance rigor.
