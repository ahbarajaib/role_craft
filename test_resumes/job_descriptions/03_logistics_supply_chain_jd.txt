# 03. SUPPLY CHAIN & LOGISTICS OPERATIONS MANAGER
Company: Nexus Global Logistics & Freight Services
Location: Full-Time | ₹10L - ₹16L / $75k - $95k

### ABOUT THE ROLE
We are looking for a Supply Chain & Logistics Operations Manager to supervise domestic and international freight operations, optimize warehouse inventory throughput, and manage 3PL carrier partnerships. You will lead cost-reduction initiatives, improve On-Time In-Full (OTIF) delivery metrics, and ensure trade compliance across multi-modal distribution channels.

### KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Oversee daily freight forwarding operations across air, ocean, and ground transport networks, monitoring transit times and customs clearance.
- Manage warehouse storage and fulfillment operations (100,000+ sq. ft.), enforcing FIFO/LIFO inventory management and cycle counting standards.
- Negotiate competitive contract rates and service-level agreements (SLAs) with 3PL providers, freight forwarders, and drayage carriers.
- Optimize inventory turnover, reduce warehouse carrying costs, and eliminate product shrinkage and damage.
- Prepare and verify international shipping documentation: Commercial Invoices, Packing Lists, Bills of Lading (BOL), Certificates of Origin, and Customs Declarations.
- Ensure strict adherence to trade compliance, Incoterms 2020, hazardous material regulations (HAZMAT/DOT), and customs audit requirements.
- Leverage WMS/TMS platforms to analyze route efficiency, fuel surcharges, and carrier performance metrics.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS & COMPETENCIES
- Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Operations, or related discipline. CSCP or CLTD certification is an advantage.
- 4 to 7 years in freight forwarding, distribution center operations, or end-to-end supply chain management.
- Hands-on expertise in Warehouse Management Systems (WMS) and Transportation Management Systems (TMS) such as SAP MM/WM, Oracle SCM, or Manhattan Associates.
- Comprehensive knowledge of Incoterms 2020, customs brokerage, tariff codes (HS codes), and import/export compliance.
- Proven analytical ability using Excel/PowerBI to track inventory accuracy, demurrage reduction, and freight cost per unit.
- Strong negotiation and problem-solving skills under tight delivery deadlines.
