# 01. SENIOR FINANCIAL ACCOUNTANT
Company: Global Vertex Enterprises | Financial Services Division
Location: Hybrid / Remote | Full-Time | ₹12L - ₹18L / $85k - $110k

### ABOUT THE ROLE
We are seeking an experienced and analytical Senior Financial Accountant to lead our general ledger operations, financial reporting, and compliance functions. In this role, you will manage end-to-end month-end close cycles, complex balance sheet reconciliations, internal audit controls, and statutory tax filings. You will partner directly with our Finance Controller and VP of Finance to ensure strict GAAP/IFRS adherence and optimize working capital.

### KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Lead the full-cycle month-end, quarter-end, and year-end financial close processes, ensuring accuracy and timely delivery within a 5-day close window.
- Perform and review complex general ledger reconciliations, intercompany balance eliminations, accruals, prepaid expenses, and fixed asset depreciation schedules.
- Oversee Accounts Payable (AP) and Accounts Receivable (AR) operations, implementing internal control workflows that reduce aging balances and DSO (Days Sales Outstanding).
- Prepare comprehensive financial statements (Balance Sheet, P&L, Cash Flow Statements) and variance analysis reports for executive leadership and external auditors.
- Ensure strict compliance with US GAAP, IFRS, local statutory tax regulations (GST/VAT/TDS, corporate income tax), and Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) internal controls.
- Coordinate with external audit firms during annual financial audits, preparing audit work papers, schedules, and technical accounting memos.
- Drive automation in ERP workflows to eliminate manual journal entries and streamline financial reporting.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS & COMPETENCIES
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field. CPA, CMA, or CA qualification strongly preferred.
- 4 to 8 years of progressive accounting experience in corporate finance, public accounting, or mid-to-large enterprise environments.
- Deep expertise in US GAAP / IFRS standards, revenue recognition principles, and double-entry accounting mechanics.
- Advanced proficiency in Enterprise ERP software (NetSuite, SAP S/4HANA, QuickBooks Enterprise, Oracle Financials).
- Advanced Microsoft Excel capabilities (VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, Nested IFs, Pivot Tables, Power Query, Financial Modeling).
- Strong experience with variance analysis, working capital optimization, and financial statement consolidation.
- Exceptional attention to detail, analytical problem-solving, and communication skills for cross-departmental presentations.
