# 10. EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT TO C-SUITE & OFFICE ADMINISTRATOR
Company: Crescent Point Holdings | Corporate Executive Suite
Location: On-site / Full-Time | ₹7L - ₹12L / $65k - $85k

### ABOUT THE ROLE
We are seeking a proactive, highly organized Executive Assistant & Office Administrator to provide comprehensive administrative and operational support to our CEO, Managing Director, and Board of Directors. You will manage complex global travel, calendar coordination, confidential board correspondence, meeting minutes, and corporate office administration.

### KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Manage complex, fast-changing executive calendars, scheduling high-priority internal meetings, investor briefings, and international video conferences.
- Coordinate end-to-end domestic and international executive travel itineraries: flights, luxury hotel bookings, visa documentation, and ground transportation.
- Prepare executive slide presentations, board meeting briefing packets, financial summaries, and official correspondence.
- Attend executive leadership meetings, recording accurate, actionable meeting minutes and tracking deliverables across department heads.
- Reconcile corporate credit card expenses, review executive receipts, and submit monthly reimbursement reports in Concur/Expensify.
- Supervise corporate office administration: facility management, front desk operations, office supply procurement, and vendor contract maintenance.
- Maintain utmost discretion, confidentiality, and professional integrity regarding sensitive corporate affairs, M&A discussions, and HR matters.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS & COMPETENCIES
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Communications, English, or related discipline (CAP / PACE certification preferred).
- 4 to 8 years supporting C-level executives (CEO, President, VP) in fast-paced corporate environments.
- Advanced mastery of Microsoft Office 365 (Word, PowerPoint, Excel, Outlook) and Google Workspace.
- Exceptional calendar logistics, timezone coordination, and international travel management capabilities.
- Stellar written English, proofreading precision, and polished professional etiquette.
- Superb multitasking and calm problem-solving composure under tight deadlines.
