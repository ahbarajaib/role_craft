# 04. SENIOR HR GENERALIST & TALENT OPERATIONS LEAD
Company: Horizon Infotech Global
Location: Hybrid | Full-Time | ₹9L - ₹15L / $70k - $90k

### ABOUT THE ROLE
We are seeking an experienced Senior HR Generalist & Talent Operations Lead to direct people operations, talent acquisition, employee relations, and HR policy compliance across a 400+ person organization. In this role, you will champion employee engagement, streamline performance appraisal cycles, and ensure complete legal and statutory compliance.

### KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Lead end-to-end recruitment for critical technical, leadership, and operational roles, collaborating with hiring managers on job architecture and scorecards.
- Direct new hire onboarding, orientation workflows, and employee lifecycle management to ensure 90-day retention and ramp-up.
- Administer bi-annual performance appraisal review cycles, KPI goal alignment, and promotion frameworks.
- Mediate employee grievances, conduct workplace investigations, and foster an inclusive, high-performance company culture.
- Ensure comprehensive statutory HR compliance: Labor laws, PF/ESI/Gratuity, POSH compliance, FLSA/EEOC regulations, and company handbook policies.
- Partner with payroll and finance teams to audit monthly payroll inputs, compensation bands, benefits administration, and leave records.
- Track and report on core HR metrics: Cost-per-Hire, Time-to-Fill, Employee Net Promoter Score (eNPS), and Voluntary Turnover.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS & COMPETENCIES
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Human Resources, Psychology, or Business Administration (SHRM-CP / PHR / SPHR preferred).
- 4 to 7 years of full-cycle HR operations, talent acquisition, and employee relations experience.
- Proficiency in enterprise HRIS and ATS platforms: Workday, BambooHR, Darwinbox, Greenhouse, Lever, or Zoho People.
- Deep understanding of labor employment laws, statutory payroll filings, and employee disciplinary protocols.
- High emotional intelligence, conflict resolution capability, and executive presentation competence.
- Strong spreadsheet and reporting skills for people analytics and workforce planning.
