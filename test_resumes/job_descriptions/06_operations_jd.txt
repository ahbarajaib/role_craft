# 06. BUSINESS OPERATIONS MANAGER
Company: Vantage Growth Partners
Location: Full-Time | ₹14L - ₹22L / $95k - $125k

### ABOUT THE ROLE
We are looking for a strategic and execution-focused Business Operations Manager to streamline organizational workflows, eliminate operational bottlenecks, lead cross-functional transformation projects, and scale daily business infrastructure. You will work side-by-side with our COO and Department Heads to measure KPIs and drive operational efficiency.

### KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Evaluate and optimize company-wide standard operating procedures (SOPs), identifying automation opportunities to reduce overhead costs.
- Lead cross-departmental operations initiatives across sales, customer delivery, supply chain, and IT.
- Design, deploy, and maintain executive business dashboards in PowerBI/Tableau to track operational throughput and resource allocation.
- Manage vendor procurement contracts, office facilities, operational tooling budgets, and SaaS software stack governance.
- Facilitate quarterly business reviews (QBRs), OKR tracking, and strategic headcount planning across business units.
- Implement Lean Six Sigma or Agile frameworks to standardize project delivery cycles and eliminate operational redundancies.
- Formulate business continuity and risk mitigation plans for mission-critical operations.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS & COMPETENCIES
- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Engineering, Operations, or related field (MBA or Lean Six Sigma Green/Black Belt preferred).
- 5 to 9 years of experience in business operations, management consulting, operational strategy, or scaling mid-to-large business units.
- Advanced quantitative and analytical skillset (PowerBI, Tableau, SQL, Advanced Excel, Process Flowcharts in Lucidchart/Visio).
- Demonstrated experience managing operational budgets (₹5Cr+ / $1M+) and negotiating enterprise vendor agreements.
- Exceptional stakeholder management, change management, and project execution capabilities.
