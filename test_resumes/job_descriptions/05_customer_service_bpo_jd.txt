# 05. CUSTOMER SUPPORT OPERATIONS & TEAM LEAD
Company: TransactPro BPO & Global Customer Success
Location: Full-Time / Shifts | ₹6L - ₹10L / $50k - $70k

### ABOUT THE ROLE
We are hiring a Customer Support Operations Team Lead to manage a 25+ agent omnichannel contact center team (voice, chat, email, and social support). You will drive SLA adherence, coach frontline support agents, resolve high-tier escalations, and elevate customer satisfaction (CSAT) and First Contact Resolution (FCR) metrics.

### KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Supervise day-to-day inbound/outbound support operations, managing shift scheduling, queue load balancing, and real-time adherence.
- Maintain and exceed organizational KPI targets: CSAT > 92%, First Contact Resolution (FCR) > 85%, and Average Handling Time (AHT) optimization.
- Conduct weekly Quality Assurance (QA) audits on recorded calls and ticket transcripts, delivering constructive coaching to underperforming reps.
- Handle complex tier-3 customer escalations, executive complaints, and critical billing/technical incident resolutions.
- Author, update, and maintain internal knowledge base (KB) articles, troubleshooting playbooks, and standardized macro templates.
- Analyze ticket trend data to identify recurring product bugs or customer friction points, reporting findings to Product Engineering.
- Lead monthly agent training on active listening, empathy, de-escalation techniques, and product feature updates.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS & COMPETENCIES
- 3 to 6 years in customer service, call center operations, or BPO environment, with at least 1-2 years in a team lead/supervisor role.
- Mastery of customer support platforms: Zendesk, Freshdesk, Salesforce Service Cloud, Intercom, or Jira Service Management.
- Familiarity with telephony and workforce management software: Avaya, Five9, Genesys, Talkdesk, or Dialpad.
- Proven track record coaching teams to meet strict SLA response times and customer satisfaction benchmarks.
- Stellar crisis management, de-escalation, and written/verbal English communication skills.
