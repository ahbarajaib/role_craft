# 02. B2B ENTERPRISE ACCOUNT EXECUTIVE
Company: Apex Cloud Technologies | Enterprise SaaS Division
Location: On-site / Hybrid | Full-Time | ₹15L - ₹25L + Uncapped Commissions

### ABOUT THE ROLE
We are hiring a high-performing B2B Enterprise Account Executive to drive net-new revenue, manage strategic enterprise accounts, and execute complex multi-stakeholder sales cycles. You will prospect mid-market and Fortune 500 decision-makers (CTOs, CIOs, Heads of Procurement), qualify pipeline through MEDDIC/BANT frameworks, and close 6-figure annual contracts.

### KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Own the entire outbound sales lifecycle from discovery, solution demo, and proof-of-concept (POC) to commercial negotiation and executive sign-off.
- Build, manage, and maintain a qualified pipeline equal to 3-4x of your annual quota (₹1.5Cr+ / $1M+ ARR).
- Conduct consultative discovery calls to identify prospect operational pain points and map our enterprise software capabilities.
- Partner with Solutions Engineers to craft tailored enterprise product demos and technical RFP/RFI responses.
- Champion competitive positioning, pricing strategies, and executive presentations with C-level stakeholders.
- Maintain meticulous CRM data integrity in Salesforce/HubSpot, tracking stage progression, win rates, and pipeline health.
- Collaborate with Customer Success to ensure smooth enterprise onboarding and expansion opportunities.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS & COMPETENCIES
- 4+ years of quota-carrying B2B enterprise sales or SaaS account executive experience.
- Verifiable track record of exceeding 100%+ of annual sales targets and closing enterprise deals.
- Proficiency in structured sales methodologies: MEDDIC, Challenger Sale, Command of the Message, or BANT.
- Hands-on mastery of sales enablement tools: Salesforce CRM, HubSpot, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ZoomInfo, Outreach/Salesloft.
- Strong contract negotiation, objection handling, and executive closing acumen.
- Outstanding verbal articulation, presentation design, and high-stakes communication skills.
